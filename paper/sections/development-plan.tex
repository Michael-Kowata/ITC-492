\section{Development and Documentation Plan}
M1 establishes scope and the project foundation. M2 evaluates upload, OCR,
authorization, and storage choices. M3 implements extraction and review; M4
creates the roster and individual forms; M5 addresses links and recovery.
M6 evaluates and documents the system, and M7 prepares final delivery. Milestone
dates are planning targets, not evidence that these activities are complete.

The October 28, 2026 revision distinguishes the original semester calendar from
the development schedule. Repository-based foundation work began September 14,
and M1 completed October 28. With the owner's authorization to extend development,
M2--M7 receive sequential two-week planning windows from October 29, 2026 through
January 20, 2027. Original targets remain in the schedule history. This is a
project plan, not confirmation of an academic deadline extension.
