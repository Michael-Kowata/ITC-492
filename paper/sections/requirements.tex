\subsection{Attendance Priority}
The project owner specified that IT Support and Instructor entries are excluded
from the student roster. The suffix \texttt{- IP} denotes in-person attendance
and is preserved in roster names but omitted from copied Form titles and Drive
filenames. Under the current naming
convention, entries without the suffix represent online students, subject to
instructor review. The baseline creates and lists in-person entries first,
retaining screenshot order within each group and stable entry identifiers.
The instructor administers in-person tests first through manual link
distribution; creation order is not an access restriction or timed-release
mechanism. The current fixture has 19 visible rows and an expected roster of
17 students, including two in-person students. These are reference expectations,
not measured OCR or Form-generation results.

\subsection{Approval and Privacy}
Copied Form titles and Drive filenames will use student name followed by a
separator and the template Form title, removing only the student's trailing
\texttt{- IP}. Preserve internal hyphens and roster attendance metadata.
For example, \texttt{Riley Carter - IP} becomes
\texttt{Riley Carter - OSHA Final Exam} for template \texttt{OSHA Final Exam}.
Snapshot the template title for retries and leave the template unchanged.
Duplicate titles still require distinct Form IDs. This is planned behavior.

The control spreadsheet is the working surface; the generated roster is a
separate deliverable. Cancellation before approval must not create an output
roster or student forms. Temporary OCR resources may already exist and must
follow a documented cleanup policy on success, failure, and cancellation.

Real screenshots, rosters, credentials, and exported run state must not enter
version control. Local ignore rules support this policy but do not replace
review of staged files. Cloud permissions, retention, and cleanup mechanisms
remain design decisions to validate. A form bearing a student's name does not
authenticate the respondent; access settings must be tested separately.
